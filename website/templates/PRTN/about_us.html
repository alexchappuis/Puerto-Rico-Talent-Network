About Us

A talent firm built for Puerto Rico, by people who believe in it.

Our Mission

Puerto Rico Talent Network is a talent search and recruiting firm connecting exceptional professionals with companies building and hiring on the island.

We partner with organizations committed to creating outstanding professional opportunities and strengthening Puerto Rico's growing business and technology ecosystem.

Our approach is relationship-driven and long-term. We work closely with both companies and candidates to ensure strong alignment — supporting lasting success for everyone we partner with.

Meet the Founder

Carmen Muñoz

Carmen grew up in Puerto Rico working alongside her father at his regional hardware store. She learned early what it takes to build something — and what it takes to keep it running.

She studied international business with a minor in translation at American University. After graduating, she launched a company exporting Puerto Rico's local fruit juices to American grocery stores.

In her mid-twenties she moved to the Bay Area, where she spent the next thirty years — right in the middle of the largest technology talent boom in the world.

Now Carmen is back in Puerto Rico. She started PRTN to bring everything she learned and everyone she knows back to the island — helping it grow into the talent powerhouse it has the potential of becoming.
